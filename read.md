# AccessTransit project status

AccessTransit is an implemented Flutter prototype with local automated validation. Production readiness, deployed rules, two-device delivery and physical accessibility verification are not established.

Use [README.md](README.md) for setup, [feature-inventory.md](docs/feature-inventory.md) for tested capability boundaries and [fix-progress.md](docs/audit/fix-progress.md) for dated implementation/test/build evidence.

Spoken TTS/voice guidance, automatic stop/background notifications, emergency contact, walking/quiet-route ranking and ticket purchase/redemption are unavailable. Operator access requires trusted approval/assignment. Fares are prototype estimates; timetable labels are separate from GPS evidence. A membership card is not a transit ticket.

The root [QA test plan](Sprint1_QA_Test_Plan.docx) and [QA tracker](Sprint1_QA_Tracker.xlsx) are present tracked files. Their contents/current coverage/sign-off remain unreviewed in this reconciliation. Do not claim completed QA merely from their filenames.

Participant research, design/testing evidence, pitch deck, poster, required videos, team records and signed reflections remain pending or unsupplied. No participant results or feature completion are inferred from plans. The original audit is a historical snapshot; the fix log records subsequent corrections, including the QA-file inventory correction.
