# AccessTransit capability inventory

Reconciled during F26 on 2026-10-15 against the working tree, local test evidence and artifact inventory. **Implemented** below means connected application code with local automated coverage. It does not mean production deployment, participant validation or successful delivery on real devices. The original audit remains a snapshot; use [fix progress](audit/fix-progress.md) for current validation/rollout status.

## Implemented with local coverage

- **Authentication/profile:** email/password registration/login, existing-profile preservation and reset-link handling. Operator registration is an application role, not trusted approval. Access and bus assignment require reviewed backend approval. Evidence: [auth service](../lib/services/auth_service.dart), [registration tests](../test/registration_profile_preservation_test.dart), [reset-link tests](../test/password_reset_link_handler_test.dart), [operator policy](operator-approval.md).
- **Passenger planning:** direct buses, boarding/alighting segment, date/time and ordered overnight schedules; endpoint-scoped wheelchair/step-free evidence, typed result tabs and explicit empty-state requirement review. Results are timetable estimates. Fares are prototype duration/stop estimates, not official tariffs. Evidence: [planning tests](../test/passenger_journey_plan_test.dart), [recovery tests](../test/accessible_empty_recovery_test.dart), [scheduling](scheduling.md), [fare policy](fare-estimates.md), [fare flow tests](../test/fare_disclosure_test.dart).
- **Journey selection/save/resume:** results to evidence, explicit passenger confirmation, acknowledged persistence and owner-scoped resume. Saving does not book a seat, confirm the bus is operating or create GPS/equipment verification. Evidence: [flow tests](../test/journey_persistence_flow_test.dart), [transaction tests](../test/operator_transaction_test.dart).
- **GPS/operator trips:** approved assigned operators can start trips, share acknowledged GPS/condition changes and record arrivals/completion. Fresh matching-bus receipts can support current GPS status; saved/expired/unavailable telemetry is distinct. Local GPS fixes are not automatically shared. Background reliability remains unverified. Evidence: [telemetry tests](../test/telemetry_cache_invalidation_test.dart), [operator transaction tests](../test/operator_transaction_test.dart).
- **Assistance:** reviewed boarding/alighting target, acknowledged request delivery/status, operator acceptance/decline/completion and passenger cancellation. Sending is not acceptance. No push-notification or response-time guarantee. Evidence: [delivery policy](assistance-delivery.md), [delivery tests](../test/assistance_delivery_test.dart).
- **Community evidence:** public report/photo publication after acknowledgement, pending/failed retries, confirm/flag/resolve transactions, dated cooldown rechecks, freshness updates and Unknown/barrier states. Malformed timestamps cannot become fresh evidence. Evidence: [publication](report-publication.md), [mutations](../test/report_mutation_delivery_test.dart), [rechecks](../test/report_recheck_test.dart), [freshness](report-freshness.md).
- **Local storage/preferences:** bounded report retention/removal and account cleanup; Android private encrypted outbox, session-only drafts on other platforms. Display controls are device settings; travel needs are per UID with guest defaults. Evidence: [outbox policy](report-outbox-privacy.md), [privacy tests](../test/report_outbox_privacy_test.dart), [preference policy](accessibility-preferences.md), [account tests](../test/account_preferences_test.dart).
- **Display/accessibility:** high contrast, large tap targets, supported text scaling and selected semantics/contrast/touch-target tests. This is partial automated coverage, not complete WCAG compliance or tested TalkBack usability. Evidence: [contrast tests](../test/wcag_color_contrast_test.dart), [touch-target tests](../test/wcag_touch_targets_test.dart).
- **Membership/rewards:** informational membership-card/profile UI and trusted manual reward reconciliation tooling. Counters are server/admin controlled; no monetary value, paid ticket, validated transport entitlement or redeemable partner offer is established. Evidence: [reward policy](rewards.md).

## Unavailable or future ideas

The app has no connected spoken TTS/voice navigation, speech/AI travel assistant, automatic stop/proximity/background notifications, emergency contact action, automatic assistance preference, measured walking-route ranking, noise/quiet-route matching, transfer routing, Sinhala/Tamil localization, offline map download, ticket purchase/payment/redemption or NFC pass validation. Disabled controls and planned ideas are not completed features. See [unsupported preferences](accessibility-feature-support.md). Offline acknowledged catalogue caching does not mean offline live GPS/maps or delivery.

Onboarding is an illustrative walkthrough. Its ETA and assistance preview labels are examples, not actual service/driver receipts. The copy describes unavailable voice guidance, evidence review, conditional GPS and the difference between requesting assistance and receiving an operator response. It does not promise stress-free travel or automatic rewards.
